generated notices are in [`THIRD-PARTY.md`](../THIRD-PARTY.md); this page is the short form.

The importer added **no new crate dependencies**. It reuses `reqwest`, `serde`, `tokio`,
`futures`, `percent-encoding`, the `oauth2` crate already used for the playback sign-in, and the
existing parsing code. The unused `librespot-oauth` workspace dependency and its
`[patch.crates-io]` entry are not present, because nothing references that crate.

Notable obligations:

- **MPL-2.0** crates (Symphonia decoders, `option-ext`, `priority-queue`) are used unmodified;
  their source is available from crates.io and the upstream repositories linked in
  `THIRD-PARTY.md`.
- **GPL-3.0-or-later** crates remain under the GPL; the combined work is distributed under the
  same licence.
- The **Widevine CDM is not shipped** in any build. The widevine host only talks to a CDM the
  listener installed separately.
- Provider names, marks and service links belong to their owners. The Spotify, Apple Music,
  YouTube Music, Tidal, Deezer, Subsonic and Jellyfin marks are used only to identify the
  service the listener connected.

## Reproducing the notices

```powershell
python scripts/generate-notices.py
```

The script requires `cargo-about` (`cargo install cargo-about --features cli`) and rewrites
`THIRD-PARTY.md` from the resolved dependency graph. The checked-in file was generated by that
script; do not edit it by hand.

## What this report does not cover

- A legal review of every transitive licence combination.
- The licences of services reached over the network (Spotify, Audius, Jamendo, lyric providers,
  Discord, scrobble servers). Their terms govern their own data.
- Code-signing or trademark clearance for the Soundy name and icon.
